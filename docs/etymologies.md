# Etymologies of the words Avik raises

Every word Avik raises explicitly in discussion, with its etymology as `hyperactive etym` gives it
(Wiktionary, followed to its deepest recorded root). The words are his; agents' own words do not go
here. `docs/word-list.md` is a separate file, whose lines Avik gives himself.

- mental ⟨Middle French mental … ← Proto-Indo-European *méntis “thought”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- weird ⟨Old English wyrd “fate” … ← Proto-Indo-European *wert- “to turn”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- novelty ⟨Old French novelté “novelty (something that is new)” … ← Proto-Indo-European *néwos “new”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- navigation ⟨Middle French navigation … ← Proto-Indo-European *néh₂us “boat”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- social ⟨Middle French social “allied (on the same side)” … ← Proto-Indo-European *sekʷ- “to follow”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- collaborate ⟨French collaborateur “collaborator” … ← Old Latin labōs “work”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- cooperate ⟨Late Latin cooperātus “having worked together with another; having cooperated” … ← Proto-Indo-European *h₃ep- “to work”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- communicate ⟨Latin commūnicāt- “share, impart; make common” … ← Proto-Indo-European *mey- “to change”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- intention ⟨Old French entencion “intention; intent; aim” … ← Proto-Indo-European *ten- “to stretch, draw”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- work ⟨Old English weorc “work, in various senses, including:” … ← Proto-Indo-European *wérǵom “work”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- act ⟨Old French acte … ← Proto-Indo-European *h₂eǵ- “to drive”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- together ⟨Old English tōgædere “together” … ← Proto-Indo-European *gʰedʰ- “to join, to unite”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- routine ⟨French routine “routine (all senses)” … ← Latin rupta [via] “a path made by force”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- relation ⟨Anglo-Norman relacioun “report; retelling of events” … ← Proto-Indo-European *bʰer- “to bear, carry”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- rationale ⟨Late Latin ratiōnāle “nominative/vocative/accusative singular neuter of …” … ← Proto-Indo-European *h₂er- “to put together”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- script ⟨Old French escrit “past participle of escrire” … ← Proto-Indo-European *(s)kreybʰ- “to scratch”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- scene ⟨Middle French scene “stage (location where a play, etc., takes place)” … ← Proto-Indo-European *(s)ḱeh₃- “shade, shadow”⟩  (raised 2026-11-14/15)
- interaction ⟨inter- "between" + Latin āctiō, from agere "to drive, to do" ← Proto-Indo-European *h₂eǵ- "to drive"⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- hyperinteractivity ⟨Greek hyper "over, beyond" + interaction + -ive + -ity: driving between, beyond measure⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- intelligence ⟨Latin intelligentia, from intelligere = inter "between" + legere "to gather, to read, to choose" ← Proto-Indo-European *leǵ- "to gather"; the twin of interaction (inter + agere)⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- primitive ⟨Latin prīmitīvus "first of its kind", from prīmus "first"⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- domain ⟨Old French demaine, from Latin dominium "ownership, lordship", from dominus "lord, master of the house", domus "house"⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- knowledge ⟨Middle English knowleche, from Old English cnāwan "to know" ← Proto-Indo-European *ǵneh₃- "to know", the root of Greek gnōsis and Latin (g)nōscere⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- ignorance ⟨Latin ignōrantia, from ignōrāre "not to know", from in- "not" + gnārus "knowing" ← Proto-Indo-European *ǵneh₃- "to know": the same root as knowledge, negated⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- cut ⟨Middle English cutten, of uncertain origin, probably North Germanic⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- controversy ⟨Latin contrōversia, from contrōversus "turned toward the opposite side", contrā + versus "turned", from vertere "to turn"; in Roman rhetoric a controversia was the exercise of arguing a case from both sides⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
- consensus ⟨Latin cōnsēnsus, from cōnsentīre "to feel together, to agree", con- + sentīre "to feel, to perceive"; the Latin counterpart of Greek syn- + aisthēsis⟩  (raised 2026-11-15)
